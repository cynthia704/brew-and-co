Our Story

We believe great coffee starts long before it reaches your cup — and that a great coffee shop becomes part of your daily life.

How It Started

A Kitchen Table Dream

In 2015, two friends — Maya Torres and James Kim — started grinding, brewing, and obsessing over coffee in Maya's Brooklyn apartment. What began as weekend experiments with a hand grinder and a Chemex turned into a shared vision: to bring genuinely exceptional coffee to their neighborhood without the pretentiousness.

They saved for two years, traveled to coffee farms in Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala, and opened the first Brew & Co. location on Maple Street with just four tables, a La Marzocca espresso machine, and an unwavering belief that great coffee builds community.

Nine years later, that belief still drives everything we do.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Three principles guide every decision we make — from sourcing beans to designing our spaces.

🌿

Radical Transparency

We publish the name, region, and price paid to every farmer we source from. No secrets. No middlemen. We believe you deserve to know the full story behind your cup.

🤝

Community First

Our shops are designed to be third places — not just coffee stops. We host open-mic nights, local art exhibitions, and free community cuppings every month.

♻️

Sustain the Planet

100% compostable cups and packaging. Used coffee grounds composted with local urban farms. Solar panels on our roastery. We're aiming for carbon neutrality by 2027.

Milestones

Our Journey

2015

Opened the Maple Street flagship in Brooklyn

Four tables. One espresso machine. Infinite ambition.

2017

Won Best Coffee Shop — Brooklyn Magazine

Two years in, the neighborhood had spoken. This was the beginning.

2019

Opened our in-house micro-roastery

Full control from green bean to finished cup. Roasting every Thursday — you can smell it from three blocks away.

2021

Expanded to 4 Brooklyn locations

Fort Greene, Park Slope, and DUMBO joined the family. Same soul, same standards.

2023

Launched Brew Lab — brewing education program

Monthly classes teaching customers to brew exceptional coffee at home. Now with 1,200+ alumni.

2026

You're here. Let's keep growing. ☕

500,000+ drinks served. 22-person team. Still obsessing over every detail.
